Enter WR numbers associated with the layout.
  • Must start with 5 digit WR number
  • Separate multiple WR numbers with a comma (,), forwardslash (/), or hyphen (-)
  • ‘–’ : To indicate a range
    • 12345-7 for 12345, 12346, 12347
    • 12349-51 for 12349, 12350, 12351
    • 12345-12347 for 12345, 12346, 12347
  • ‘/’ : To separate multiple WR numbers
    • 12345/7 for 12345, 12347
    • 12385/415 for 12345,12415
    • 12349/18273/71621 for 12349,18273,71621
  • ‘,’ : To separate multiple WR numbers
    • WR number after ',' must be 5 digits
    • 12349,18273,71621 for 12349,18273,71621
